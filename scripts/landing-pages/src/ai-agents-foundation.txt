h2 Four agents. One team.
p Not four separate tools. Four agents that hand work to each other, so what one produces, the next one uses. Together, they form your AI Operating System.
img M/6acca587c478ac5535bd59c4.png AI content generation: text, image and video
h3 Content Agent: your marketer
p “Turn this product brief into a week of social posts, with visuals.”
p AI drafts the copy in your voice, generates matching images, voice-overs and short-form video, ready for you to review. <i>brief → copy → images → video</i>
img M/6acca586bbad531ac8a62236.png AI data analysis: charts and dashboards
h3 Data Agent: your analyst
p “Here's last quarter's sales sheet. What changed, and why?”
p AI surfaces the patterns and anomalies, you verify every number, and it becomes a chart and a one-slide summary. <i>spreadsheet → insights → verify → dashboard</i>
img M/6acca587bd6bb710ca61a615.png AI automation: a workflow with a gear
h3 Automation Agent: your operations assistant
p “Every time an invoice hits my inbox, file it and log it.”
p You build an n8n workflow that reads each invoice, files it in Drive and updates the sheet. Then it runs on its own. <i>Gmail → n8n → Drive → Sheets</i>
img M/6acca587bd6bb710ca61a60f.png Vibe coding: a code editor window
h3 Builder Agent: your web developer
p “Build me a booking page for my car service shop.”
p You describe it in plain words. AI writes the code, and you publish a working page with a link you can share the same day. <i>idea → Claude → live on Netlify</i>
