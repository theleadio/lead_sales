h1 What You Will Learn
img M/6a021748b543ed771ed240d3.png
h3 1. Get better results from Claude
p Learn how Claude responds to instructions, context and examples. Write clearer prompts and choose the right model for the task, so you spend less time correcting vague or unusable answers.
img M/6a021751a3dd25aa2a412c96.png
h3 2. Choose the right Claude tool
p Understand how Claude Chat, Artifacts, Skills and Cowork fit together. You will know which tool to use when you need a document, dashboard, presentation, website or repeatable workflow.
img M/6a021759a2398e6af2d909a4.png
h3 3. Claude Code: Boost Development &amp; Work Efficiency
p Use plain English to create landing pages, dashboards and productivity tools, even if you have no coding background. Learn how to improve the result and publish a working website through GitHub Pages or Netlify.
img M/6a02175e60a7a52fdc007c4e.png
h3 4. Hand multi-step work to Claude Cowork
p Give Cowork the relevant files and describe the finished result you need. Use it to turn receipts into expense sheets, organise research into a clear brief and handle recurring work through routines.
img M/6a021787b543ed771ed24fc8.png
h3 5. Connect Claude to the tools you use
p Extend Claude with Skills and connectors. Practise a Notion workflow that researches a topic, organises the findings and saves them directly into your database, reducing the need to copy and paste between apps.
img M/6a02176fb7ca635d75d4dfdf.png
h3 6. Use Claude for real business work
p Combine prompts, Skills, Cowork and connectors to complete practical work. Apply the same approach to reports, presentations, content, research and everyday office tasks after the workshop.
