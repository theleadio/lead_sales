# lead_sales

LEAD's own sales platform, replacing SalesProcess: inbox and tickets, automations, websites and forms, email marketing, social planner and payments.

Contacts, deals, classes and enrolments stay in [`lead_crm`](https://github.com/theleadio/lead_crm). This app shares its Supabase database and keeps its own tables in the `sales` schema.

The design specs are in `lead_crm` under `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-*.md` (PR #39). Start with `2026-11-04-lead-sales-build-plan.md`.
